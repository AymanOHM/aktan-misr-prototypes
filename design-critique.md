# Design critique: Aktan Misr site (phase 1)

Reviewed 2026-11-28 on branch `claude/blissful-brahmagupta-oqkng9`, build "built 21 pages". Screenshots: home, /products/, /products/bath-towels/, /manufacturing/, /quote/, /ar/, /demo/ at 1440x900 and iPhone 13, full page, plus seven steps through the mill chapter on desktop. Screenshot names below (`desk-home-chapter3`, `phone-home-01` …) refer to that set. It was saved in a session scratchpad and will not survive. To regenerate it, serve the repo's parent folder on a port and run Playwright over the same pages.

**Caveat on type:** api.fontshare.com was blocked in the review container, so every screenshot shows the fallback serif (a Times-like face), not Sentient. I judged type scale, rhythm and weight, not the Sentient face itself. See finding 8.

## Verdict

1. It is tidy, legible and restrained, and nowhere looks like slop. It does not yet look tasteful in a particular way: it reads as a well-made "warm editorial" template, not as this mill.
2. Machine-made is not the tell. Template-made is: serif on grained warm paper, text-left/mosaic-right hero, three-up card grid, zigzag photo rows, full-bleed photo with a white plate, giant "1956" numeral, coloured footer statement, everything fading up, plus a blurred colour glow. Each move is defensible alone. Together they are the 2024–26 kit, and the 2000s catalogue lifestyle photos (model in a robe with a laptop) make it read as stock.
3. The one change that matters most is to **show the mill.** No page has a photograph of a loom, the dye house, a person or El-Mahalla. The "one mill controls" story is told with lifestyle product shots and Unsplash stand-ins. Until a shoot exists, build the site's character from what only this mill has (its jacquard cloth up close, the spec sheet, the trade-fair stand), and remove the effects that stand in for it.

**What works and should stay:** the quote page's live specification sheet (`desk-quote-00`) is the most particular, useful thing on the site, and nothing else in the category does it this well. The spec tables on product pages are clear and honest. The page-head banners for inner pages are plain and fast. The nav is clear and the CTA is always visible. Text contrast passes everywhere I measured (muted on paper 5.83:1, paper on teal 9.27:1). There is no horizontal scroll: the wider full-page captures were `overflow-x: clip` hiding the halo, not real overflow.

## Strongest case for keeping the current direction

Buyers are procurement people, not design jurors. What wins the quote is clarity, credibility and a short path to the form, and the current site has all three: an obvious CTA, an honest evidence table, a specification-building quote form, bilingual pages, and no gimmick that breaks. Familiar conventions lower the effort of reading. The effects are already throttled: desktop only, reduced-motion safe, one band. The real cure, a photo shoot, is not a design change and may take months. Stripping effects now could leave a site that is plainer without being more particular.

**Why I still call it:** the strip-down costs almost nothing. Cutting the halo, the reveal and the pin is about a day, and removes the parts most likely to be read as generated. Meanwhile, the place the site loses buyers (findings 1 and 4: "is this really a mill?") is not addressed by the current direction at all. Keep the structure, the form and the palette. Remove the decoration that pretends to be the mill, and spend the effort on the photographs.

## What I did not check

- Sentient itself (Fontshare was blocked here), and whether Noto Naskh / IBM Plex Sans Arabic rendered in the AR screenshots.
- Inner pages: /private-label/, /hotels/, /distributors/, /quality/, /about/, /catalogue/, /contact/, /privacy/, other product pages, and all AR inner pages.
- The other hero options in /demo/ (Citrus, Teal dots, Sun) and the tint variants. I looked at the default state only.
- Tablet widths (60–75rem, where the chapter switches off and the nav collapses), landscape phone, and Safari/WebKit. Playwright's iPhone 13 profile is Chromium with a phone viewport.
- Keyboard and screen-reader passes, focus order in the quote form, and form submission.
- Load performance, image weights and LCP.
- Copy: left as written, per the brief. On tone, it is plain and specific, which is right for this audience.

History

The mill began weaving in El-Mahalla El-Kubra, Egypt’s textile city, in 1956. founding story, family generations

Today it weaves, dyes and finishes towels, bathrobes and pareo for export, about 10 tons of finished goods a day.

It ships to buyers in Italy, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Greece, Australia and Morocco, among others. full market list

It exhibited at Heimtextil, Frankfurt, in 2025 and 2026.

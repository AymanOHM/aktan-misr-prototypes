How private label works

From your brief to a packed container, in six steps.

  1. Brief

    Product, size, weight, colour, quantity and market.

  2. Design

    Our graphic department draws your artwork for the jacquard looms.

  3. Lab dip and sample

    Your shade matched in our dye house; sample in sample lead time.

  4. Approval

    You approve the sample, shade and label before production.

  5. Production

    Woven, dyed and finished in one mill; lead time production lead time.

  6. Label, pack and ship

    Your labels, bands and cartons; shipped for export.
